Mammoth 0.1.23

- Library now has My Library and Discover views.
- Search BitMidi's free community MIDI catalogue without leaving the app.
- Credited matching versions are grouped under one composition, with source details and evidence-based robustness ordering.
- Download a version for actual piano audio and fitted falling-note previews before importing.
- Inspected duration, note count, tracks, piano programs and declared hands; missing authors/difficulty/full-song status stay Unknown.
- Import exact Complete music with shared Recall. Repeat imports deduplicate; added versions and revisions preserve earlier arrangements and history.
- Existing songs, settings, progress and the updater's trust identity are retained.

Limitations: title-only or conflicting files remain separate when their composition identity is uncertain. Multi-instrument MIDI is not automatically converted into a proper piano arrangement. Discovery imports currently offer Complete and Recall, not automatically extracted Melody/Accompaniment. Paid provider access and broader external-page discovery are not activated in this release. Free access does not imply permission to redistribute a song.
