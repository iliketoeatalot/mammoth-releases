Mammoth 0.1.22

- One global More menu with click-open, click-away popup controls.
- Dedicated Practice with floating hand, fingering, wait and sheet controls. Scored Play runs continuously at normal tempo with both hands; practice results do not change scored bests.
- Automatic song key fitting, clickable outside-view warning, restored Focus icon, Space transport and contextual Backspace restart.
- Configurable key colour and brightness, pooled dynamic particles, brighter hit glow and larger live points with per-hit gains.
- Interactive Learn home with eight short piano exercises; expanded Career overview, repertoire and recent results.
- Editor duplicate, quantize, explicit hands, velocity and duration controls with undo; empty-field click no longer advances time.
- Expert filter removed; existing Expert pack assets retain their identities under Advanced alternate. Existing songs, settings and progress are retained.
- Factory Mammoth originals are hidden from Library while their history remains available. Added complete Mutopia public-domain editions of The Entertainer and Maple Leaf Rag with explicit right/left staves.

Learn is an initial practical lesson collection. Automatically generated practice versions and fingering remain estimates. Beatles arrangements are not bundled without a redistribution licence. Hello Day remains a separate user song pack.
