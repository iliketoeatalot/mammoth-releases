Mammoth 0.1.19

- Four piano-key mastery badges: bronze Melody, silver Accompaniment, gold Complete, platinum Recall. Names appear on hover and when selected. Existing practice history is retained.
- Library labels and filters consistently describe the source song's musical difficulty, separately from mastery. Song details show tempo and measured source-note density, with an explanation of the distinction.
- Alt-left dragging right enlarges piano keys; left reduces them. Plain click or Shift-click clears a selected repeat passage. Plain click with no selection continues to seek.
- Focus hides the header, controls, keyboard and information overlays, leaving the note field and an exit icon at the top right. Escape exits Focus.
- Uncapped points: 10 per matched note plus up to 10 timing points, with note-length-dependent Perfect windows, ordinary combos up to 8x and sustained Perfect combos up to 16x. Wrong or missed notes break both streaks; mistimed correct notes retain ordinary combo charge.
- End-of-run points use source-song difficulty multipliers 1x/2x/3x and mastery multipliers 1x/2x/3x/4x. Career XP rewards matched notes, completed full runs and improved eligible personal bests.
- Local personal bests require the whole selected arrangement at normal tempo without wait-for-notes. Guided practice receives correct-note points but no fictional Perfect timing. Online shared leaderboards are not included.
- Earlier attempts retain their original history and receive no invented past points or XP.

Example: 54 all-Perfect notes earn 8,780 raw points under scoring version 1. Hypothetical Advanced/Recall Twinkle earns 105,360; the actual Beginner/Recall arrangement earns 35,120.
