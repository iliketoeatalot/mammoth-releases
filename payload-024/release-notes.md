Mammoth 0.1.24

- Discovery separates matching titles from collapsed related results and removes unrelated songs.
- Save compositions and specific versions for later; browse saved discoveries after restarting.
- Selecting a version automatically loads its falling-note preview and MIDI analysis. Audio remains button-controlled; cached previews can be refreshed.
- Filter sources and inspected MIDI types. Playability/difficulty estimates consider distinct attacks, density, instruments and declared hands; sustain is not counted as 25 simultaneous presses.
- Simultaneous notes are visually linked. A chord earns points only with all required keys held; scored play uses an 80 ms attack window, while guided practice permits finding and holding each key.
- Save practice points and comparable personal bests per arrangement, passage, hands, tempo and assistance. Loops have their own results and do not replace scored mastery bests.
- Expected-key cues have independent colour, saturation, brightness and glow intensity. Live swatches and individual reset buttons accompany the colour controls; sliders, toggles and selectors have default buttons.
- Particle bursts respond to MIDI velocity with varied shape, speed, drag, gravity and lifetime, while respecting Calm/performance limits.
- Existing songs, settings, historical results and the updater's signing identity are preserved.

Limits: MIDI playability and difficulty are estimates rather than human certification. BitMidi remains the direct provider and lacks reliable MIDI-creator identities, so creator following is unavailable for those entries. Paid providers and author catalogue integrations are not activated in this release. Older results keep their original meaning; new comparable practice setups are recorded in result format v4.
